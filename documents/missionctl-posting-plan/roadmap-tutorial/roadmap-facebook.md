🔮 We Shipped Our Roadmap Early — Here's What's Actually Next

Back when we first wrote the missionctl roadmap, the plan was: postctl v1.0 and calctl v0.1 by Q3 2026, mailctl and budgetctl alphas by Q4 2026, notectl and taskctl by Q1 2027, and a full 9-tool, 43-MCP-tool suite by Q2 2027.

We beat that timeline by months. All 9 tools are live: mailctl, calctl, taskctl, notectl, budgetctl, habctl, timectl, diaryctl, and postctl — 66 MCP tools total (more than the 43 we originally scoped, since habctl, timectl, and diaryctl weren't even in the first draft of the plan).

Pricing landed simpler than planned, too. Instead of one flat bundle price, each tool now sells standalone: free core functionality, with a one-time $9 unlock for AI features on mailctl, calctl, budgetctl, and habctl (notectl's $9 unlocks a second vault). The full missionctl Bundle is $39 one-time, covering all 9 tools plus future updates.

What's genuinely next — not committed, just written up as proposals so far: healthctl (a terminal medication planner) and investctl (portfolio tracking). Both still need real design decisions before any code gets written.
