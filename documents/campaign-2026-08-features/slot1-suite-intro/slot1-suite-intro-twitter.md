Tired of cloud SaaS tools locking up your data just to connect AI assistants to your life?

Meet `missionctl` — a local-first suite of 9 Go tools giving AI agents "hands": email, calendar, tasks, notes, budget, habits, time, diary, social posting. 🧵
---
Each tool manages one domain locally in SQLite and exposes a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server:

📧 mailctl · 📅 calctl · 📝 taskctl · 📓 notectl · 💰 budgetctl
🔥 habctl · ⏱️ timectl · 📔 diaryctl · 🐦 postctl

66 MCP tools total, all pluggable into Claude Desktop.
---
No cloud database, no monthly subscription traps. Your credentials and history stay encrypted on your own local disk — every tool compiles to a single static Go binary.

We've been shipping a steady stream of TUI polish lately — more on that this week. 👀
