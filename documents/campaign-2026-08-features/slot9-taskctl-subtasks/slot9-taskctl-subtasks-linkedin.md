✅ Break Tasks Into Checklists: Subtasks Land in taskctl

Not every task is a single action. "Plan the launch" is really five or six smaller steps, and cramming each of those into its own top-level task just clutters the list without capturing that they belong together.

taskctl now supports subtasks — attach a checklist to any task, check items off individually, and see progress on the parent task at a glance. Fully synced with Apple Reminders (which has its own subtask/checklist concept we now map to), and still entirely keyboard-driven — no mouse required anywhere in the TUI.

Small feature, but the kind that matters once you actually use a task manager daily instead of just adding items to it.
