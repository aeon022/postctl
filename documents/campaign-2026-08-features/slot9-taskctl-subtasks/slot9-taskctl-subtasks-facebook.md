✅ Break Tasks Into Checklists: Subtasks Land in taskctl

Some tasks aren't one thing — "plan the launch" is really five or six smaller steps. taskctl now lets you attach subtasks/checklists to any task, so you can track the pieces without spawning five separate top-level tasks cluttering your list.

Check items off individually, see progress on the parent task at a glance — still fully synced with Apple Reminders, still entirely keyboard-driven in the TUI.
